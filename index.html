About Me

I am a professor of language education and research methods. I have authored over a dozen books in the field, conducted numerous research projects, and published articles in scientific journals. Currently, I serve as a full-time lecturer at the Faculty of Languages, Universitas Ma Chung, Malang. In a career that spans over 30 years, I have produced a substantial body of work.
